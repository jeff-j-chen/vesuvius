**Problem:** Eval figure generation was slow due to low batch size (32).

**Solution:** Increased `eval_infer_bs` to 256 on Linux for all campaigns. This leverages the available VRAM (16GB RTX A4000) to speed up evaluation inference without affecting training.

**System specs:**
- RAM: 503GB
- GPU: NVIDIA RTX A4000, 16GB VRAM
- Optimal batch settings from throughput sweep: bs=96, workers=12, lr=1.5e-4

**Files modified:**
